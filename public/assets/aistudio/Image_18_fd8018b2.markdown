## Brand & Style

This design system powers a zero-commission, instant on-demand marketplace connecting discerning clients with elite, verified bridal and event artisans—makeup artists, hairstylists, and photographers. The experience reconciles two traditionally opposite mental models: the stately, timeless exclusivity of an haute couture bridal atelier and the real-time velocity, transparency, and geographic utility of an on-demand dispatch network.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **Refined Editorial Luxury with Precision Utility**:
- **Tone & Persona:** Authoritative, celebratory, deeply trusted, and effortless. It strips away transactional clutter and aggressive commission funnels in favor of curated prestige, transparent pricing, and instant live booking.
- **Visual Tension:** Stately, deliberate serif headlines juxtaposed against ultra-crisp, legible functional controls, live map cards, and dynamic status radials.
- **Signature Cues:** Deep imperial depths punctuated by warm champagne-gold metallic accents, luminous soft-lavender canvas surfaces, and pill/rounded card envelopes that mimic bespoke stationery.

## Colors

The color system distributes visual weight following a deliberate 60-30-10 editorial allocation:

- **Dominant Core (60%):** `#2E165B` (Deep Royal Purple) anchors critical master elements—luxury navigation rails, hero backdrops, confirmed booking states, and primary header banners. Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) and luminous `#F8F6FC` (Soft Ivory-Lavender) provide breathing room across main canvas surfaces and card containers.
- **Structural Foundations (30%):** Border strokes leverage `#E8E2F2` for whisper-soft separation. Text employs `#181226` (Dark Charcoal) for high-contrast, editorial legibility on light surfaces, while `#5E5570` (Muted Purple Slate) communicates secondary metadata, timestamps, and subtle tooltips.
- **Accents & Live Actions (10%):** Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`, supported by dark gold `#B8972E` for pressed states and highlight `#E5C358` for shimmer gradients) is reserved exclusively for high-intent moments: primary CTA buttons, 'Verified Pro' badges, live arrival trackers, star ratings, and OTP verification inputs.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an intentional dual-engine framework:

1. **Editorial Display Layer (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for high-emotion brand touchpoints, artist nomenclature, editorial portfolio titles, and collection groupings. It infuses classic bridal elegance, warmth, and artistic craftsmanship into digital layouts.
2. **Functional System Layer (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Powers all high-frequency operational interfaces—live ETA telemetry, radar dispatch indicators, filter chips, calendar pickers, and checkout summaries. Its geometric clarity guarantees uncompromised legibility on both mobile handheld screens and desktop dashboards.

Display headings scale down gracefully using dedicated mobile tokens to prevent awkward line breaks in portrait viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model pairs a fluid responsive grid with fixed architectural constraints to preserve white space on high-resolution displays:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid with a maximum content container width of 1360px, utilizing `gutter-lg` (2rem) and `margin-lg` (3.5rem). High-density operational dashboards (such as live dispatch and multi-artist tracking) employ split-screen fixed-rail layouts (map on left/canvas on right).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `gutter-md` (1.5rem) and `margin-md` (2rem).
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column fluid grid, `gutter` (1rem) and `margin` (1rem). On-demand live status sheets conform to bottom-anchored modal drawer patterns with safe-area padding.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px modular baseline, ensuring seamless optical alignment between dense numerical badges and generous editorial hero blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids generic heavy drop shadows, relying instead on ambient illumination and refined tonal stacking:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** Soft Ivory-Lavender (`#F8F6FC`) background canvas framed with hairline borders (`1px solid #E8E2F2`).
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Artist Profiles):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle border tracing (`#E8E2F2`) and an ambient micro-shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(46, 22, 91, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Bars & Map Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border of `#E8E2F2`, elevated by `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(46, 22, 91, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Live Dispatch Drawers):** Elevated sheet interfaces utilizing soft backdrop-filters (`blur(12px)`) with `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(24, 18, 38, 0.16)`.
- **Gold Accent Sheen:** Active cards, verified pro badges, and active inputs feature a fine outer micro-glow: `0 0 0 1px #D4AF37, 0 4px 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses generous rounding to project approachability, gentleness, and tactile modern luxury:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges):** 0.5rem (8px) corner curvature.
- **Card Containers & Modals (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`):** Standard elements use 1rem (16px), while artist profile showcases, portfolio carousels, and dispatch tracking sheets use 1.5rem (24px).
- **Pill Architecture:** Filter chips, verification badges, live ETA status tags, and primary action buttons utilize full capsule pill shapes (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast against structural rectilinear map interfaces.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary CTA ("Book Artist Now", "Confirm Dispatch"):** Pill-shaped with a linear gradient from `#E5C358` to `#D4AF37`, Dark Charcoal text (`#181226`, weight 600), subtle gold inner highlight, and a smooth hover transition to dark gold (`#B8972E`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Deep Royal Purple (`#2E165B`) background with crisp white text and 12px horizontal padding.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent background with hairline border `#E8E2F2`, text `#2E165B`, shifting to soft lavender `#F8F6FC` on hover.

### Verified Pro Badges & Zero-Commission Markers
- **Verified Pro Badge:** Pill container, Deep Royal Purple background, hairline gold border (`#D4AF37`), gold icon (crown/star/check), and uppercase micro-label (`#D4AF37`, 10px, weight 700).
- **Zero-Commission Pill:** Ivory-Lavender container (`#F8F6FC`), purple slate text (`#5E5570`), featuring an emerald micro-dot denoting 100% direct-to-artist payout transparency.

### Artist Profile & Portfolio Cards
- Constructed with 1.5rem (24px) border-radius on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border (`#E8E2F2`).
- Integrated 4:5 editorial aspect-ratio media preview, floating live availability badge in top-left corner (e.g., "Available Today • 45m Dispatch"), and gold-filled rating pill in top-right.

### Form Inputs & OTP Verification
- **Input Fields:** Crisp white containers, height 48px, rounded 12px, border 1px solid `#E8E2F2`. Focus state transitions to `1px solid #D4AF37` with a matching 2px diffuse ring (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`).
- **OTP Confirmation Blocks:** 56x64px individual digit tiles, bold monospaced or sans numerals (`#181226`), active digit indicated by bottom border highlight in `#D4AF37`.

### Live Radar & Booking Tracker
- Inspired by instant live mobility tracking: soft-dimmed grayscale-and-purple map canvas, real-time artist beacon pulsating with a dual-ring gold radar effect, and a docked bottom sheet displaying artist arrival ETA, vehicle/kit details, and direct audio call trigger.
